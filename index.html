AQI Risk Guide (Official Health Advice)

AQI Range Risk Level Health Advice & Precautions
0 – 50 Good Normal outdoor activities. Minimal impact. No precautions needed.
51 – 100 Satisfactory / Moderate Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive individuals. Normal outdoor sports permitted.
101 – 200 Moderate / Poor Avoid prolonged brisk walks near heavy traffic. Wear mask if sensitive or asthmatic.
201 – 300 Poor / Unhealthy Stay indoors where possible. Use N95 mask outside and run air purifiers indoors.
301 – 400 Very Poor Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Strict N95 mask mandatory outdoors.
401 – 500 Severe Emergency measures. Avoid all outdoor activity. High health hazard for all age groups.
